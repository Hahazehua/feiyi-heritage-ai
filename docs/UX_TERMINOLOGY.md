## Wording boundaries

Prefer:

- “AI opportunity assessment” instead of “market research”
- “demo price range” instead of “price” when merchant verification is absent
- “create an inquiry” instead of “place an order”
- “submitted for review” instead of “published”
- “supported claim” instead of “proven fact” when evidence scope is limited

Avoid claims of verified artisans, merchants, inventory, shipping, production capacity, customers, orders, revenue, conversion, or market impact unless separate evidence exists.
